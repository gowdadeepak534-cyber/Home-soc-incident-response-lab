NETWORK TRAFFIC ANALYSIS

Tool: tcpdump
Evidence: Local lab packet capture

Observations:
 
* DNS related traffic was observed.
* TCP connection attempts were observed.
* ICMP destination unreachable messages were observed.

Assessment:
           Some network destinations were unreachable during the capture.
           This alone does not indicate an attack.

Limitation:
          The capture represents a short sample of lab traffic. No malicious
          activity was confirmed.
